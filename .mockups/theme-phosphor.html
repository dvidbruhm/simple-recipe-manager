08 · Recipe cook view

> classic tiramisu — header + ingredients/steps + notes + footer
Recipes
Classic Tiramisu
★★★★☆☆
A classic Italian dessert of espresso-soaked ladyfingers layered with mascarpone cream and dusted with cocoa.
dessert italian no-bake
// Ingredients
6 egg yolks
¾ cup white sugar
1⅔ cups heavy cream
1 package (around 30) ladyfingers
1¾ cups strong brewed espresso, cooled
2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
// Steps
Step 2 of 6
01Whisk egg yolks and sugar until thick and pale.
02Fold in mascarpone until smooth.
03Whip cream to soft peaks and fold in.
04Combine espresso and a splash of liqueur in a shallow dish.
05Dip each ladyfinger briefly and layer in a dish.
06Spread cream, dust with cocoa, chill 4+ hours.
Notes

Best made a day ahead. Don't oversoak the ladyfingers or the layers turn to paste.
