Classic Tiramisu

★★★★☆

A classic Italian dessert of espresso-soaked ladyfingers layered with mascarpone cream and dusted with cocoa.

dessertitalianno-bake

Ingredients

06 ITEMS
Servings
6egg yolks
¾ cupwhite sugar
1⅔ cupsheavy cream
1 pkgladyfingers (around 30)
1¾ cupsstrong brewed espresso, cooled
2 tbspunsweetened cocoa powder

Method

STEP 02 / 06
02 / 06
  1. DoneWhisk egg yolks and sugar until thick and pale.
  2. Current · Step 02Fold in mascarpone until smooth.
  3. Whip cream to soft peaks and fold in.
  4. Combine espresso and a splash of liqueur in a shallow dish.
  5. Dip each ladyfinger briefly and layer in a dish.
  6. Spread cream, dust with cocoa, chill 4+ hours.
Notes

Best made a day ahead. Don’t oversoak the ladyfingers or the layers turn to paste.
