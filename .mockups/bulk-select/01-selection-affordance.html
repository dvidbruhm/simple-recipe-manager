How do you want to select recipes?

Your cards are currently one big link — clicking opens the recipe. Bulk selection needs an affordance that doesn't fight that. Below are three approaches, each shown in its idle state (nothing selected) and its active state (selecting). All use your real theme. Hover the cards in approach A to see the reveal.

A

Hover-reveal checkbox + "Select" toolbar button recommended

Nothing shows until you interact. On desktop, hovering a card reveals a small circular check in its corner — click it to enter selection mode. A permanent Select button in the header gives an explicit, discoverable entry point that also works on touch devices.

Clicking a card now toggles selection instead of opening it.

Pros

  • Zero clutter at rest — fully unobtrusive
  • "Select" button is discoverable & touch-friendly
  • Familiar (Gmail / Drive / Files-style)

Cons

  • Hover-reveal is desktop-only (offset by the button)
  • Two entry paths to keep in sync
B

Long-press / right-click to enter mode

The cleanest at rest — absolutely no extra UI. Press and hold a card (or right-click on desktop) to enter selection mode with that card pre-selected. A floating pill bar appears. Most native-feeling on mobile; least discoverable on desktop.

Bar floats over content; tap ✕ to exit.

Pros

  • Most minimal at rest — nothing to clutter the grid
  • Feels native on mobile (Android/PWA share app)

Cons

  • Hidden gesture — desktop users may never find it
  • Right-click conflicts with browser context menu
  • Needs a visible escape hatch icon anyway
C

Always-visible corner checkbox

A small checkbox lives permanently in each card's corner. Checking any one reveals the action bar. Maximum discoverability — but it adds a persistent visual element to every card, which works against the "unobtrusive" goal.
