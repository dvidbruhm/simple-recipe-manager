How should bulk delete feel?

Try it: tap checkboxes (or Select all), then Delete. Selected cards animate out and a toast appears with a single Undo that restores all of them at once. Action bar placement — bottom vs top

Bottom (above) stays out of your header toolbar and sits under the thumb on mobile. Top replaces the toolbar — more visible but loses search/sort access mid-selection.

Why no confirm dialog

  • Consistent with your existing single-delete (toast + undo)
  • Soft-delete is fully reversible — undo restores all N at once
  • Less friction for the common "clean up a batch" case

If you'd rather confirm

  • Add a confirm step for large counts (e.g. >10) only
  • Tradeoff: interrupts flow; undo already covers safety
