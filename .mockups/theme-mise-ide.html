dessert/classic-tiramisu.md

Classic Tiramisu

★★★★★

A classic Italian dessert of espresso-soaked ladyfingers layered with mascarpone cream and dusted with cocoa.

dessert italian no-bake marmiton.org

function ingredients()

6 items
// servings
1
6 egg yolks
2
¾ cup white sugar
3
1⅔ cups heavy cream
4
1 package (around 30) ladyfingers
5
1¾ cups strong brewed espresso, cooled
6
2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder

function steps()

▶ cook mode
Step 2 of 6
1
whisk(egg_yolks, sugar) // until thick and pale
2
fold_in(mascarpone) // until smooth
3
whip(cream).to("soft peaks") // fold in
4
combine(espresso, "splash of liqueur") // shallow dish
5
dip(ladyfinger).briefly() // layer in a dish
6
spread(cream); dust("cocoa"); chill(4+ hr)

// verbatim: Whisk egg yolks and sugar until thick and pale. Fold in mascarpone until smooth. Whip cream to soft peaks and fold in. Combine espresso and a splash of liqueur in a shallow dish. Dip each ladyfinger briefly and layer in a dish. Spread cream, dust with cocoa, chill 4+ hours.

Notes Best made a day ahead. Don't oversoak the ladyfingers or the layers turn to paste.
